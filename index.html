    How to Play

    Desktop: use the mouse, or play entirely from the keyboard with Arrow keys or W/A/S/D to move the board cursor and Enter/Space to select. V switches 2D/3D, F flips, U undoes, M toggles sound, and N starts a new game. The first play input enters immersive full screen; Esc exits.

    Phone/tablet: the handheld console appears automatically. Use its D-pad and Select button, or tap the board directly. The game enters full screen from your first play input when the browser permits it.

    Legal moves, check, checkmate, castling, en passant, pawn promotion, stalemate and standard draws are enforced.
