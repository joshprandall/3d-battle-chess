Choose your battle.

Configure the match, then launch the board as a dedicated game experience. Your browser will enter full screen when it permits it; phones and tablets play in landscape.

How to play

Tap or click a piece, then a highlighted square. Desktop players can use Arrow keys or W/A/S/D to move the board cursor and Enter/Space to select. V switches 2D/3D, F flips the board, U undoes, M toggles sound, and N starts a new game.

All normal chess rules are enforced, including check, checkmate, castling, en passant, promotion, stalemate, repetition, and the fifty-move rule.
